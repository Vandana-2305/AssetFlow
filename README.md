# AssetFlow – AI-Powered Institutional Asset Lifecycle Management System

## Overview

AssetFlow is a comprehensive institutional asset management platform designed to manage the complete lifecycle of assets, from procurement requests and acquisition to verification, maintenance, repair, vendor returns, and final disposal.

The system integrates AI-powered automation, QR code tracking, and centralized inventory management to improve operational efficiency, transparency, and accountability across departments.

## Key Features

### 1. Asset Inventory Management
- Centralized asset registration and management.
- Track asset details, status, and departmental allocation.
- Maintain complete asset lifecycle records.

### 2. Procurement Management
- Manage institutional procurement requests.
- Track procurement progress and acquisition details.
- Connect procurement records with asset inventory.

### 3. Smart Requisition Import (AI)
- Import procurement requisition documents.
- Extract relevant information using AI.
- Reduce manual data entry and processing time.

### 4. Verification Workbench
- Verify newly acquired assets against procurement records.
- Identify discrepancies and record verification results.
- Streamline the asset acceptance process.

### 5. AI Defect Detection
- Analyze uploaded asset images using AI.
- Identify potential visible defects and damage.
- Support asset condition assessment and verification.

### 6. Vendor Return Workflow
- Manage defective or damaged asset returns.
- Track vendor return requests and their status.
- Maintain return-related records.

### 7. QR Code Asset Tracking
- Generate unique QR codes for registered assets.
- Scan codes to retrieve asset information.
- Simplify asset identification and tracking.

### 8. Audit Logging
- Maintain records of important system activities.
- Track changes and operations across modules.
- Improve transparency and accountability.

## Asset Lifecycle Workflow

AssetFlow connects all modules through a structured lifecycle:

1. Procurement Request
2. Smart Requisition Import
3. Procurement Processing
4. Asset Registration
5. Asset Verification
6. AI-Based Defect Detection
7. QR Code Tracking
8. Maintenance, Repair, or Vendor Return
9. Final Asset Disposal

## AI Integration

AI enhances the platform through two major capabilities:

- **Smart Requisition Import:** Automatically extracts procurement information from uploaded documents.
- **AI Defect Detection:** Analyzes asset images to identify potential defects and assist verification decisions.
